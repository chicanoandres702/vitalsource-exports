Move beyond first impressions. See art in everyday life.

Art is part of our lives, from the monuments in our communities, to the fashions we wear and the media images we take in, to the exhibits on display in museums and galleries. It permeates our daily life. But why do we study art? How do we talk about art?

Living with Art helps students see art in everyday life by fostering a greater understanding and appreciation of art. Taking a step further, the authors equip students with the tools necessary to analyze and digest the art that surrounds them, and uphold a life-long enthusiasm for art.

Understand ART

This edition models the use of art’s vocabulary throughout the text, using key terms so that readers understand their applications and drawing attention to where the visual element, principle of design, or characteristic of the medium is most visible in the object. The text tells the story of art and teaches readers to see that story in the book’s images and in the world around them.

In addition to the text, resources and assessments help develop students’ understanding:

  • SmartBook with learning resources reinforces concepts, models visual analysis, and draws thematic, cultural, and historical connections as students learn about art and its vocabulary.

  • Practice and review activities challenge students to apply their comprehension of concepts to works of art from within and outside of the text.

  • Elements and principles interactive media and art process videos offer an engaging introduction to core concepts and the vocabulary of art to build comprehension and bring students into the creative process.

Analyze ART

Connect delivers assessments, analytics, and resources—including SmartBook and Writing Assignment—that make Living with Art a rich learning experience. Featured activities that help students analyze art include:

  • Guided viewing exercises, which feature links to Google’s Arts & Culture site. Questions guide students through learning the process of describing what they see, providing formal analysis of various works of art, discussing their meanings, and ultimately developing informed opinions.

  • Thematic writing prompts, which encourage students to explore gallery or museum collections in person or online. The prompts guide them in making connections between works of art by choosing a theme, looking for works that reflect this theme, and supporting their selections with formal and contextual details.

Appreciate ART

Living with Art continues to promote inclusivity, with many women artists among the artists featured, along with representations of art from around the world. The new edition continues to integrate global and diverse art works from the Middle East, Latin America, Africa, Australia, and Asia, as well as increasing the representation of Native American, Black American, and Latino/a art.

The featured Artists essays are tied closely to the concepts and issues discussed in the body of their respective chapters.

Thinking about Art essays focus on the social, historical, and global context, introducing issues of art over time—how art has been appreciated, interpreted, destroyed, categorized, displayed, fought over, preserved, censored, owned, and studied.

Chapter 3 of Living with Art helps students appreciate some of the common themes of art. The following themes include writing prompts and are further explored in the eBook and SmartBook’s learning resources in Chapters 14 to 23:

  • The Sacred Realm

  • Politics and the Social Order

  • Stories and Histories

  • Picturing the Here and Now

  • Reflecting on the Self

  • Invention and Fantasy

  • The Natural World

  • Art about Art and Its Institutions
